Coffee, literally. Light roast, no milk, no sugar. Black means black.

I've tried something like fifty roasters so far, mostly Indian, mostly single origin, mostly in pursuit of the fruit. If a coffee needs milk to be drinkable, it's worth asking why, and the answer is usually that it was roasted dark to hide something.

The one opinion of mine that starts arguments: a filter coffee at a Bangalore darshini and a pourover of a Chikmagalur natural are both excellent and have nothing to do with each other. People keep asking for a ranking. There won't be one.
