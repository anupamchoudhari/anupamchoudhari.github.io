I build AI products. I have been doing it for about fifteen years, twice as a co-founder, and most of that time has gone into the unglamorous half of the problem: getting something that demos well to survive contact with how an organisation actually works.

These days the work is agent harnesses. Not the model, not the prompt, the scaffolding around them that decides what the agent can see, what it is allowed to do, when it should stop and ask, and how you know whether any of it worked. The interesting problems in AI products have moved from capability to permission, evaluation and trust, and almost none of that is visible in a demo.

A thread that runs through more of my work than I planned: graphs of skills, occupations and traits, and what they let you say about who can do what. I keep arriving back at it from different directions, which at this point I take as a signal rather than a coincidence.

## How I work

- **Write the decision down, including the bet you lost.** A roadmap that only records what shipped is a press release.
- **Show the thing.** A working prototype that is wrong is more useful than a document that is right.
- **Argue about the definition of correct before arguing about the approach.** Most AI projects fail at the evaluation set, not the model.
- **Be specific in public.** Nobody learns anything from "driving impact at scale".

## Before this

I spent four years in the United States, from 2007 to 2011. A master's in computer science at RIT in Rochester, which taught me what a real winter is, and a stretch at Los Alamos, which is the only place I have worked where the scenery was the most distracting thing in the building. Between the two I drove an unreasonable amount of the country. There is an easter egg on this site for the road trips; you will find it or you won't.

Home is Bangalore now, fifteen years of it. Before that Thane and Mumbai, which is where I am from in the sense that matters. My in-laws are in Kanpur, so I get a third India a few times a year, and it corrects any theory I have been developing about the first two.

I have a daughter. She does not appear on this website, by design, but a fair amount of what is in Parents Corner exists because of her.
