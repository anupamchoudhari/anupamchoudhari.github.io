Light roast, no milk, no sugar. Black means black.

I have worked through something like fifty roasters by now, mostly Indian ones, mostly single origin, mostly in pursuit of the fruit. If a coffee needs milk to be drinkable I would rather know why, and the honest answer is usually that it was roasted dark to hide something. This page is the log: what I bought, what it tasted like, and whether I bought it again, which is the only review score that means anything.

A note on the only opinion here that gets me into arguments: a filter coffee at a Bangalore darshini and a pourover of a Chikmagalur natural are both excellent and have nothing to do with each other. People keep asking me to rank them. I keep refusing.
